Approval Atlas data files (https://approval-atlas.vrognas.com)

These JSON files are built daily from public sources. meta.json lists each source with its
version, retrieval date, license and attribution.

Source: European Medicines Agency (EMA), https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/download-medicine-data
(medicines data, EPAR documents index and orphan designations): the medicines EMA lists from
its central procedure, whatever their status. © EMA. Filtered to human medicines and reshaped. Not affiliated with or endorsed by EMA.

Other sources, with their own terms:
- Union Register of medicinal products, © European Union,
  https://ec.europa.eu/health/documents/community-register/html/index_en.htm, licensed
  CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); modified: matched to EMA records.
- ATC codes and names © WHO Collaborating Centre for Drug Statistics Methodology, Oslo
  (https://atcddd.fhi.no), reproduced verbatim; use requires reference to the WHOCC, no
  commercial distribution, no changes (https://atcddd.fhi.no/copyright_disclaimer/).
- MeSH® courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine (version in meta.json); not endorsed
  by NLM.
- ChEMBL data is from https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl (release in meta.json), licensed
  CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Mendez D. et al., Nucleic
  Acids Res. 2019;47(D1):D930-D940, doi:10.1093/nar/gky1075. ChEMBL gives molecule types
  and maps ATC names (WHO's, verbatim).
- LEI data: Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF), CC0. GLEIF does not provide or
  endorse this data.
- WHO INN stems (World Health Organization, 2024), read as facts in Approval Atlas's own words.
